JG Mobile Mechanic V3.4

NEW
- Returning customers now have a prominent “Request Service / Repair” option after login.
- Customers choose one of their saved vehicles.
- Request types: Service, MOT Preparation, Brakes, Suspension, Diagnostics, Repair / Problem, Other.
- Customer enters current mileage, details and optional photos.
- Request appears in the mechanic Customer Requests dashboard and can be converted to a Job.
- Customer can see their submitted requests and status in “My Requests”.
- Submitted mileage updates the saved vehicle mileage.

UPDATE
1. Run upgrade_v3_4.sql once in Supabase SQL Editor.
2. Replace the previous app files with this V3.4 package.
3. Sign in as a customer and press “Request Service / Repair”.
4. Submit a test request.
5. Sign in as mechanic and confirm it appears under Customer Requests.
